**Choose Do to work on one task.** The app shows that task and the time until you finish it, stop or move it to another day. When you stop, you can write down where you were, and that note is there the next time. If you leave the app, the task stays in view: as a Live Activity on iPhone and iPad, and as a small panel on the Mac.
