**Fixes for undo, account switching and list refresh.** You can undo deleting a project everywhere, and undo a date change made in the task editor. Signing in with another account clears the previous account's tasks from the device. On iPhone, the list redraws after it fetches changes. On the Mac, Show completed tasks applies to Today too.
