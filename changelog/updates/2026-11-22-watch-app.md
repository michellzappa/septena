**The Apple Watch app works with your Septena account.** Today opens first. Tap the circle to complete a task, with Undo, or swipe to move it to tomorrow. Dictate a new task into the Inbox, Today or Tomorrow. The Lists screen has Upcoming, Anytime, Starred, Repeating, the Logbook and your areas and projects. Changes you make offline sync later. A compass complication puts Septena on your watch face.
