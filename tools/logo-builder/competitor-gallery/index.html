Strategic White-Space Takeaways for Septena

1. Escape the Blue-Checkmark Swamp

18 of 40 apps (45%) — and 15 of 18 Core + Secondary task apps (83%) — use a literal checkmark. Eleven apps sit in Blue alone. Any flat blue checkmark instantly disappears next to Microsoft To Do, Any.do, TickTick, Google Tasks, DailyTask, Tasks.org and Stuff.

2. Why Things 3 Still Stands Alone

Things 3 uses a blue checkmark too — yet it dominates visually because it is an architectural physical object (a sculpted blue desk tray holding a recessed paper card with real light & shadow). Only 8 of 40 apps (20%) invest in tactile 3D materiality (Things 3, Superlist, OmniFocus, Linear, Obsidian, Wunderlist, Amie, Stuff). The first seven are design-cult favorites; Stuff shows that depth alone does not escape the blue-checkmark crowd.

3. Wide-Open Territory for Septena

Zero Core apps own Green / Teal, Warm Amber / Terracotta, or Warm Linen / Deep Ink. Only 2 of 40 apps use a warm paper/linen surface (Weft, Wunderlist), and zero Agent-native apps have Apple-grade tactile craft. A sculptural, non-checkmark object or geometric mark in warm amber, forest/emerald, or deep ink + tactile warmth owns untouched ground.
